About

THE WALL is a place to say the thing you can’t say anywhere else. No names, no profiles, no audience to perform for.

Community guidelines

Say anything about your life. Don’t name, target or expose other people. No personal info, handles or contact details. Be kind in the comments.

Privacy

This is an early prototype. There is no server: everything you post stays in this browser and nobody else can see it yet.
